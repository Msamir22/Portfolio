Managed Receiving

Capstone Logistics April 2020 Live Site

Enterprise inbound scheduling and receiving platform used across 600+ locations. I contribute to scalable Angular architecture, reusable UI systems, real-time data handling, performance optimization, and technical delivery across complex logistics workflows.

Project Overview

Managed Receiving coordinates inbound appointments, dock and receiving activity, real-time operational visibility, and reporting for Capstone Logistics' customers. The platform is used across 600+ locations and integrates with Yard Management workflows to help teams respond to changing arrival times and dock capacity.

My Role

  • Contribute to Angular UI architecture and component design
  • Build reusable shared components and front-end patterns
  • Implement reactive and real-time data handling with RxJS
  • Contributed to 60-70% application performance improvements
  • Participate in feature analysis, technical design, and pre-sprint estimation
  • Mentor 3 junior/associate front-end engineers through code reviews, debugging support, and Angular guidance
  • Act as a frontend technical point of contact for technical decisions and developer unblocking

Business Impact

Capstone publicly documents a grocery-wholesaler deployment of Managed Receiving that improved on-time performance by more than 80% and delivered $647K in annual savings.
